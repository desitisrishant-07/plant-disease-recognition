Bacterial Tomato

Tomato Early Blight

Alternaria solani

Concentric dark brown rings forming target-board spots on lower mature leaves, leading to defoliation.

Cure Plan

Apply copper fungicides; prune lower infected foliage.

Fungal Potato

Potato Late Blight

Phytophthora infestans

Water-soaked dark lesions with white fungal fuzz underneath during damp weather conditions.

Cure Plan

Spray systemic fungicides (Mancozeb); destroy infected stalks.

Fungal Apple

Apple Black Rot

Botryosphaeria obtusa

Small purple circular leaf spots expanding into 'frog-eye' necrotic ring patterns.

Cure Plan

Prune dead wood and apply Captan sprays during spring.

Fungal Grape

Grape Black Rot

Guignardia bidwellii

Reddish-brown leaf spots containing microscopic black spore-producing structures.

Cure Plan

Manage canopy airflow and apply Myclobutanil early.

Fungal Corn

Corn Common Rust

Puccinia sorghi

Elongated cinnamon-brown powdery pustules scattered across both leaf surfaces.

Cure Plan

Plant resistant corn hybrids; use protective foliar fungicides.

Bacterial Pepper

Pepper Bacterial Spot

Xanthomonas campestris

Small, dark water-soaked leaf spots turning brown and causing severe leaf yellowing.

Cure Plan

Use certified disease-free seeds and copper sprays.

Fungal Cherry

Cherry Powdery Mildew

Podosphaera clandestina

White powdery fungal patches developing on young leaves, causing curling and distortion.

Cure Plan

Angular purple-brown spots on leaves resulting in shot-hole appearance as tissue drops out.

Cure Plan
